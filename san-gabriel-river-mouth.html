Why this entrance gets its own model

Direction matters
South through southwest swell receives the highest mouth-exposure weighting; poorly aligned swell is discounted.
Period matters
Long-period swell receives an extra breaker-risk multiplier even when offshore wave height looks modest.
Protected fallback remains available
A failed ocean-exit check does not automatically cancel the trip. The inside-water decision is scored separately.
Visual inspection is mandatory
A forecast GO only means approach and inspect. Breaking waves across the usable channel, standing waves, a strong lateral jet or no obvious return line override the model.

Hard visual override before crossing

Do not exit if any are present: breaking waves span the practical entrance line; repeated waves are breaking between the jetties rather than only outside; visible standing waves or strong opposing current; strong lateral sweep toward either jetty; you cannot identify a clean re-entry line; conditions are materially worse than forecast. Observe multiple sets from protected water before committing.

Data and validation

Live forecast: Open-Meteo Weather + Marine APIs using total wave plus primary/secondary/tertiary swell height, direction and period. Tide timing: NOAA CO-OPS station 9410660. Directional observation references for manual validation: CDIP/NDBC San Pedro 092/46222 and San Pedro South 213/46253. The buoy observations validate incoming offshore swell; they do not measure breaking conditions inside this mouth.

Open-Meteo marine forecasts use DWD and NCEP wave-model data. This module deliberately does not turn modeled nearshore current into a hard safety threshold because model grid data is not a measurement of the jetty entrance current.
